# FOMO Radar + Recommended Stack: Simple Instructional Guide

**Goal**: Find Solana memecoins where smart money / KOL attention is arriving *early*, analyze them quickly with real data, cross-check with multiple free sources, and only act when signals align. All without paying for expensive APIs.

This follows the stack recommended by @goatyishere: Start with FOMO (fomo.family), then use RH Trenches, FOMO Pulse, Stalkchain, Hoodwatch, and the FOMO Tracking Bot for edge.

**FOMO Radar** is your free deep-analysis tool for any Solana mint (paste CA → dual Opportunity/Exit Risk scores + 7 radar cards + real Pump.fun social comments + watchlist).

## Why This Workflow Works (Simple Version)
- **Discovery tools** (Hot list in Radar, Stalkchain KOL Feed, RH Trenches/FOMO Pulse tapes, Hoodwatch) = "What are the smart/KOL/whale traders buying right now?"
- **Radar analysis** = Turn a random token into clear, numbers-based intelligence (is momentum building? Is risk low? Is real attention happening on Pump?).
- **Cross-check links** (built into Radar) + watchlist + alerts = Validate across sources and don't miss moves.
- **Result**: You spot early, analyze properly, reduce noise, and have an edge using only free/public data.

**Core Loop (5-10 minutes per candidate)**:
1. Spot interesting low-cap tokens (via Hot button or browsing tools).
2. Paste the mint into Radar and read the scores.
3. Cross-check the same token using the links in Radar.
4. Add promising ones to My Tokens + set an alert.
5. Monitor and only consider entering when Radar + at least 1-2 other sources look good.

**Key Rule**: High Opportunity + Low Risk + confirmation from another tool (e.g. KOL buys on Stalkchain) is much stronger than any single signal. Never treat Radar as "buy now" — it's intelligence.

## The Tools (What + Why)
- **FOMO Radar** (your site): Full analysis for Solana tokens. Uses free DexScreener + real Pump.fun comments for social. Shows Opportunity vs Risk gauges, radar cards, timeline, and provenance (real data vs demo).
- **Hot Low-Cap button in Radar**: One-click list of current low-cap Pump.fun tokens with MC and comment counts. Perfect starting point for Solana candidates.
- **Stalkchain (KOL Feed)**: Real-time buys/sells by known KOLs and smart wallets on Solana. Green "Bought" on low-cap tokens by high-follower or proven KOLs = strong attention signal. Use for "smart money is here".
- **RH Trenches + FOMO Pulse**: Live tapes of top fomo.family traders buying/selling (mostly on Robinhood Chain). Great for seeing what the crowd is piling into right now. Note: Often different chain than standard Solana pump.fun.
- **Hoodwatch**: Whale flows and holdings on tokens. Shows if big money is accumulating.
- **fomo.family**: Social trading app with leaderboards, feeds, and alerts. The "social layer" of the stack.
- **FOMO Tracking Bot (TG)**: Set alerts so you get notified on moves without staring at screens.

### Step 2: Find Candidates (Discovery)
In Radar, click the green **"Hot Low-Cap (Pump.fun)"** button.

![Hot Low-Cap button and list](https://fomo-radar site screenshot showing green button + list of tokens like TE, shork, PORK, SATOSHI with MC and comments)

(Alternative: Browse Stalkchain KOL Feed or RH Trenches for tokens being bought by tracked wallets.)

### Step 3: Analyze in Radar
Click **"Analyze in Radar"** on a token from the hot list (or paste any mint and hit Analyze).

Example result for a token (like the $SITCOM analysis in your screenshots):

- Gauges: Opportunity 57 / Exit Risk 16 → decent early signal, low risk.

  Dexscreener · Stalkchain (KOLs) · FOMO Pulse (RH tape) · RH Trenches · Hoodwatch · fomo.family

**What to look for in Radar**:
- Opportunity meaningfully higher than Risk.
- Early Momentum or Pre-FOMO stage (not Distribution).
- Building signals across multiple cards (volume + social + holders).
- Real Pump comments (the free social signal).

**Why**: Gives you orthogonal scores (Opportunity vs Risk) + context so you don't just chase hype.

### Step 4: Cross-Check with Companion Links
Right in the Radar result, click the links (they open in new tabs).

**Stalkchain KOL Feed example** (from your screenshot):
![Stalkchain KOL Feed](stalkchain.com/kol-feed screenshot)

**What you're looking for here**:
- Green "Bought" rows on the token or similar low-caps.
- Buys by KOLs with decent followers or from the leaderboards.
- Multiple KOLs piling into the same token quickly.
- Check the token name, amount, and "Trade" if you want to dig deeper.

**Why**: Confirms whether real KOLs/smart wallets are accumulating (leading indicator). If Stalkchain shows buys but Radar shows high Risk, be cautious.

Do the same quick check on:
- RH Trenches / FOMO Pulse (look for big buys by high-follower traders on the tape).
- Hoodwatch (whale inflows).
- fomo.family (social/leaderboard context).

### Step 5: Add to Watchlist + Alerts
In Radar:
- Click **"+ Add to My Tokens"** for anything that looks good.
- Use **Refresh All** later to see if scores improve.

Set alerts:
- Use the FOMO Tracking Bot on Telegram.
- Or alerts inside fomo.family.

**Why**: Turns one-off analysis into ongoing monitoring without constant work.

### Step 6: Decide & Monitor
Only consider entering when:
- Radar shows good Opportunity / low Risk + Early Momentum.
- At least one discovery tool (Stalkchain KOL buys, big tape buys, whale flow) confirms.
- Social (Pump comments) is rising.

Re-analyze or refresh the watchlist as price/volume moves.
